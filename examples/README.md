# Examples

Worked, annotated material that complements the guide. The guide teaches the *theory*; this folder teaches the *pattern recognition*.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`trade-walkthroughs.md`](trade-walkthroughs.md) | 8 worked trades — 2 per setup (#1 sweep+CHoCH+OB, #2 Silver Bullet, #3 Power of 3, #4 breaker+FVG). Each includes ASCII chart, framework attribution, entry/SL/TP numbers, R-result, and a one-line lesson. |
| [`ascii-charts/`](ascii-charts/) | Reusable text-mode templates of the canonical setup anatomies. |
| [`journal-template.md`](journal-template.md) | A blank trade-journal row template — copy & fill per trade, per the journaling guidance in [`../guide/09-risk-management.md`](../guide/09-risk-management.md). |

## How to study these

1. **Read** [`trade-walkthroughs.md`](trade-walkthroughs.md) once, end to end. Notice that each setup follows the **same 5-step framework** ([`../guide/04-hybrid-framework.md`](../guide/04-hybrid-framework.md)) — only the *trigger* differs.
2. **Cover the right column** with your hand and try to predict the decision from each row's observation. This builds the "if I see X, I do Y" reflex.
3. **Open TradingView's Bar Replay** on a chart you know (EURUSD M15 is a fine first symbol). Scrub backward, then play forward bar by bar, and try to call setups *before* they complete.
4. **Open the journal template** and write your first 10 trades into it — even paper trades. By trade #20 you will see your own patterns more clearly than any backtest.

## Adding your own walkthroughs

When you find an interesting historical setup on your charts, document it here. Suggested filename convention:

```
walkthrough-{symbol}-{timeframe}-{YYYYMMDD}.md
```

Use the same column structure as [`trade-walkthroughs.md`](trade-walkthroughs.md). Drop the chart screenshot into [`../guide/diagrams/`](../guide/diagrams/) and link it from your walkthrough.
